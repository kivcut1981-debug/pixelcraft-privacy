PixelCraft Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · Developer: Shafiq Ahmad · kivcut1981@gmail.com

1. Introduction

PixelCraft Pro (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a photo editing and creative studio app for Android (package name com.pixelcraftpro.textstudio), developed by Shafiq Ahmad (“the developer”). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

2. Summary

Your photos stay on your device. Editing, filters, text, layers, drawing, collage, templates, background removal, background change, batch processing and exports all run on your phone. We have no servers, no user accounts, and we do not collect, sell or share your photos or personal details.

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device information as described in section 5.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account or sign in. We (the developer) do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, microphone or camera data. The app does not request those permissions. The only data collected in connection with the app is by Google's advertising SDK, as described in section 5. We do not upload your photos, projects or exports to any server owned by us.

4. Information stored on your device

Photos you choose. You pick photos with the Android photo picker or your gallery app (and fonts with the file picker). The app reads only the files you select.

Projects and settings. Saved projects, favourite colours and small settings (for example whether you have seen the welcome guide) are stored locally in the app's own storage (IndexedDB / local storage). This data never leaves your device through the app. If Android’s Google backup is turned on for your phone, Android itself may include the app’s data in that backup; the app does not control this.

Exports. When you save an image, it is written to your gallery (Pictures → PixelCraft Pro). If you use Share, the image is handed to the app you choose; we do not receive it.

Background tools. Smart Cutout, Remove Background (No AI) and Add Background work entirely on your device, including the built-in colours, gradients, patterns and scenes. A photo you choose as your own background is used only on your device.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, rectangle and full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process your device's Advertising ID, IP address, device and network information, app and ad interaction data, and approximate (not precise GPS) location derived from your IP address. The developer does not receive this data in identifiable form, and the app does not access your precise location. This is done by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent using Google’s consent form before any ads are requested. Where this form applies, you can change your choice later from Ad privacy settings at the bottom of the home screen (this link is shown only where it is required). In other regions you can control ad personalisation through Android settings, as described below.

Your choices. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the path can vary by phone). You will still see ads, but they will be less personalised.

The app declares the Android advertising-ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) for this purpose.

6. Internet access and third-party services

The app uses the internet for ads and to load some resources it needs to work. These are ordinary web requests, so the servers can see your IP address and basic device information, but never your photos:

• Libraries (ZIP and QR-code tools) from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net).
• Fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com).
• AI Background Remove, Smart Object and Background Blur download an open-source AI model library from esm.sh and the model files from the library's public model host (IMG.LY, the maker of the open-source @imgly/background-removal library) the first time you use them. The model may be cached by the app after download and runs on your device. Your photo is not uploaded.

These services have their own privacy policies. If you are offline, some tools may not work.

7. Permissions

• Internet / network state — for ads and the resources in section 6.
• Advertising ID — for ads (section 5).
• Storage (older Android versions only) — to save your edited images to the gallery. On newer Android versions no storage permission is requested; the app uses the system photo/file picker and media store.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, call log, SMS or location.

8. Data retention and deletion

We do not hold your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server. Data on your phone stays until you remove it: delete a project inside the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app (Android Settings → Apps → PixelCraft Pro → Storage → Clear data). Images you exported to your gallery remain until you delete them yourself. For ad data held by Google, use the controls in section 5.

9. Children's privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over (or the minimum age in your country) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown are provided by Google AdMob. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

10. Security

Because your photos and projects stay on your device, their security depends on your phone's own protection (screen lock, updates). Our own code does not transmit them. No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or California law), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. Since we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is generally nothing to access or delete on our side. For ad-related data held by Google, you can use Google's tools and the Android settings in section 5. You may also contact us at the email below with any request or question.

12. International users

Google and other third parties named above may process data in countries other than your own, under their own policies and safeguards.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version will appear in the app (About → Privacy Policy) and at the privacy policy link on the store listing, with a new effective date. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
